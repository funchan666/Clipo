This Privacy Policy explains how the operator named above ("Clipo," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the Clipo iOS application and related services. For privacy questions, support, or requests concerning your information, email **[CONTACT_EMAIL]**.

## 1. Information We Handle

### Guest account and app information

When you choose one-tap sign-in, Clipo sends an app-generated installation identifier and the app identifier to our service to create or access a guest account. We receive a user identifier and authentication credentials. Guest sign-in does not require you to provide your name, email address, or phone number.

To request app configuration and account services, Clipo also sends information such as the app version, iOS platform designation, preferred language, time zone, and enabled keyboard language codes. Keyboard language codes describe language settings; they are not the text you type in other apps.

An installation identifier or guest account may still qualify as personal information even when it does not contain your real name.

### Links, searches, and online content requests

When you open a link, browse a channel, or search for videos, we process the link or short code, search terms, channel or content identifiers, and related account and request information. If a link requires an access password, the password you enter is sent to the link-resolution service to process that request.

Servers and content providers handling these requests also receive network information, including your IP address and ordinary request metadata. When you stream a video or load its cover image, the server hosting that content receives the request. Please do not submit links, access passwords, or other information you are not authorized to use.

### Local videos and library information

Videos you select from Photos or Files are copied into Clipo's local workspace. Editing, preview generation, video export, and audio extraction are performed on your device. These editing features do not upload your imported media to Clipo's servers.

Clipo stores imported files, exports, saved-video references, recently opened links, and associated titles and dates on your device. Saving an online video to your collection stores a reference; it does not necessarily create an offline copy of that video.

If you choose to share a file or link, the destination app or service you select receives it and handles it under its own privacy practices. Local app data may also be included in device backups depending on your Apple and device settings.

### Purchases and viewing allowances

Apple processes in-app payments. Clipo processes product identifiers, order and transaction information, purchase receipts, subscription status, and account associations to validate purchases, provide membership access, and handle purchase recovery. Clipo does not receive your full payment-card number or Apple Account password.

The app stores viewing and saving allowances, relevant content identifiers, and rewarded-ad completion records associated with your guest account to manage available access.

### Advertising information

Clipo integrates Google AdMob for optional rewarded advertisements. Google's advertising SDK may process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from an IP address, device or app identifiers, advertising interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. The information processed depends on the SDK configuration, your region, your permissions, and your privacy choices.

Advertising providers use relevant information to deliver and measure advertisements, prevent fraud, and maintain their services. Device identifiers, including the advertising identifier when available with the required permission, may be used for advertising purposes. Non-personalized advertising can still involve limited data processing.

Learn more in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

### Communications

If you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you include, such as your guest account ID, purchase details, or screenshots. Please avoid sending payment-card details, passwords, or unrelated sensitive information.

You may withdraw consent through the applicable privacy controls. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already lawfully performed. ## 5. Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing services, resolving requests or disputes, protecting security, and meeting legal obligations.

Account information is retained while needed to maintain the account and associated services. Links, search requests, and operational records are retained only as necessary to provide requested functionality and support legitimate operational or security needs. Purchase and accounting information may need to be retained for the periods required by applicable law, even after an account deletion request.

When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it. If a legal obligation prevents immediate deletion, we retain only the necessary information for that obligation. Local files remain under your control through the app and your device settings; copies held by independent services are subject to their own retention practices.
